About Me

I'm Md Fuad Hossain Saad, a Junior Software & Web Developer at Mariners Group and Computer Science & Engineering graduate from Daffodil International University (DIU) with solid professional expertise in modern full-stack web engineering, cross-platform mobile apps, cloud infrastructure, and AI research.

In my role at Mariners Group, I actively develop production web platforms with Next.js & Node.js, architect relational and real-time databases using Supabase & MySQL, build cross-platform mobile apps for both Android & Apple iOS with Flutter, and manage hosting and server environments across AWS & Hostinger VPS.

I have experience in published research involving Machine Learning, Association Rule Mining, Deep Learning, and predictive analytics. Skilled in Python, data analysis, research methodologies, technical documentation, and academic project development.

Alongside research, I have proven leadership and organizational acumen, having served as Vice President of the DIU Robotics Club (DIURC), and as Campus Ambassador for BUET Robotics Society, ICT Olympiad Bangladesh, Techfest IIT Bombay, and Q-bit Learning.

I am a responsible, detail-oriented individual with strong communication, teamwork, and leadership abilities, dedicated to solving real-world challenges through data-driven and AI-assisted innovations.
